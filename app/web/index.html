Strong controlled scores can hide transfer failures.

All four classifiers score above 99.5% on the held-out PlantVillage test set. Performance is substantially lower on the mapped PlantDoc and PlantSeg external subsets.

SCIENTIFIC LIMITS

Transparent about what the model can’t establish.

Softmax scores are not calibrated probabilities. Grad-CAM++ is qualitative attribution. External evaluation subsets are curated and are not a representative sample of farm conditions.
